HELP & SUPPORT

How can we help?

Practical notes for using the tools in this workspace.

⌕

Using the AI scanner

Upload a clear leaf image in JPG, PNG, or WebP format (up to 10 MB). The bundled model classifies bell pepper, potato, and tomato classes only.

▣

Your privacy

Scans and uploaded images are stored under your account. Other farmer accounts cannot access them. You can delete an analysis and its files from its detail page.

✳

Interpreting results

Confidence is the classifier’s score for its selected class, not a medical-style certainty score. Verify symptoms and ask a local agricultural expert before treatment.

⌁

Farm records

Health status is an observation you enter, irrigation is a manually maintained schedule, and soil readings are labeled as manually recorded.
