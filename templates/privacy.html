Privacy information

Deployment-specific draft — review and update before public production use.

Information the application handles

Account details include your name, email address, optional phone number and location, plus a securely hashed password. Farm, field, crop, irrigation, soil and crop-analysis records are associated with your account.

Uploaded crop images and generated reports are stored by the application under its configured instance storage. The database and file storage are controlled by the person operating this deployment.

External services

Weather lookup uses Open-Meteo and sends the requested location to that provider. Password-reset email is sent only when the operator configures SMTP. Contact messages are sent to the configured support address only when both support and SMTP settings are available.

Access and retention

Application routes scope private farm and detection records to the signed-in account. Data remains in the configured database and storage until removed by the account holder through available controls or by the deployment operator.

Deployment contact

No operator contact address is currently published by the application. Contact your deployment administrator for privacy questions and data requests.

Before deployment, the operator should confirm the applicable retention period, hosting region, backup practices and contact details.
