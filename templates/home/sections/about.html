ABOUT CROPFARMAI

Practical crop insight, connected to the rest of the farm.

CropFarmAI brings the existing crop-image classifier together with farm, field, crop and care records in one private workspace.

It is designed to help people review a model result and keep useful context nearby—not to replace an agronomist or promise a diagnosis.

Make crop-screening tools and everyday farm records easier to access and understand.

Our vision

Support more informed crop-care decisions by bringing image screening and farm context together.
