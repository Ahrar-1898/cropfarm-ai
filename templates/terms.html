Terms of use

Deployment-specific draft — review with the responsible operator before public production use.

Informational crop screening

CropFarmAI displays results from an image-classification model. A prediction or confidence score is not a confirmed diagnosis, treatment prescription or guarantee of crop outcome. Verify important decisions with a qualified local agricultural professional.

Accounts and records

Provide accurate account information, protect your login credentials and use only accounts you are authorized to access. Do not upload images or information you do not have permission to use.

Service limits

Availability depends on the deployment, configured database, model files and external providers. Weather information may be unavailable. Irrigation and soil entries are records only; CropFarmAI does not control physical equipment or connected sensors.

Operator and contact

The person operating this deployment is responsible for its configuration, data retention and support. Contact details must be supplied by that operator before these terms are used as a production policy.
